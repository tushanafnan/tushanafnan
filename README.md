## About me

I'm **Md. Sultanul Arefin Afnan**, also known as **Tushan**. I build and maintain web applications, backend services, and the systems behind them. My work includes translating business requirements into technical plans, designing APIs, troubleshooting production issues, and coordinating delivery with other developers.

I currently work as a **Lead Software Engineer at Dalian Penghui International Trading** and am pursuing an **MSc in Software Engineering at Dalian University of Technology**. Alongside application engineering, I research machine learning and blockchain and am developing a deeper focus on AI engineering and intelligent systems.

**What I focus on:** Full-stack engineering · Backend architecture · Product delivery · Applied machine learning
